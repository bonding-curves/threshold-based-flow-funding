# Threshold-Based Flow Funding (TBFF) — Requirements & Protocol Document

## 1. Experiment Overview & Objectives

**Threshold-based Flow Funding** is a framework arising from the MycoFi movement. The core mechanism involves each participant defining a **minimum floor** (required for meaningful work) and a **maximum ceiling** (beyond which funds automatically flow outward). 

Unlike traditional P2P models, TBFF utilizes a **Common Pool** as a central clearinghouse to ensure network-wide resilience.

### 1.1 The Ecological Question
*Can a human network act like a healthy forest, using a common nutrient pool to sustain everyone, or will a few large trees shade out the rest of the grove?*

**The "Canopy Shadow" Theory ($H_0$):** 
Without a mediated pool, resources will simply pool at the largest nodes, leaving smaller saplings to wither in the shade.

**The "Mycelial Pulse" Theory ($H_1$):**
A healthy ecosystem uses a central mycelial pool to move nutrients from areas of abundance to areas of scarcity. By setting "Saturation Points," we allow excess nutrients to flow into the **Common Pool**, which then prioritizes filling the floors of the entire grove.

### 1.2 What we’re trying to observe
- **Pool-Mediated Translocation:** Does the "overflow" actually reach the edges of the forest via the central reservoir?
- **Symbiotic Entry:** Can we grow the forest by bringing in new life that is sustained by the common pool?
- **Metabolic Rate:** How fast is our common reservoir consumed, and can the system become self-sustaining?

## 2. Experiment Parameters

### 2.1 Phase 1: Mini-Network Flows
- **Duration:** 3 Months
- **Scale:** 1 Network, 6 Participants
- **Growth:** Closed membrane (6 participants)
- **Visibility:** Full Financial Transparency (Tiers to be defined)
- **Funding:** Philanthropic seed funding (Direct to Common Pool)
