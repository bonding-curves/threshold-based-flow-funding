# Visual Prompts: The Anderson Mechanism (Common Pool Edition)

Use these technical prompts for Shawn's engineering visualizations.

## 1. The Hub-and-Spoke Vector Space
> **Prompt:** A minimal, technical 3D schematic of a central node (The Pool) connected to six peripheral nodes (The Grove). Arrows show the bidirectional flow of nutrients: from peripheral nodes to the center (Exudation) and from the center to the periphery (Distribution). Technical schematic, CAD style, glowing lines on black, high contrast.

## 2. The Reservoir State (Accumulation)
> **Prompt:** A professional data visualization of a central tank filling with liquid light. Six bar graphs around the tank show the "Sustainability Floors." As the tank fills, the bars light up one by one in green. Data science UI, clean, high resolution, minimalist, dark mode.

## 3. The Recursive Redistribution Pulse
> **Prompt:** A high-speed digital animation freeze-frame. A central sphere of light is pulsing outward in concentric rings toward six nodes. Each ring represents a recursive translocation step. Mathematical art, vector fields, glowing particle physics, clean and precise.
