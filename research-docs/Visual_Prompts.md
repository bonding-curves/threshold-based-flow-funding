# Visual Prompts: The Common Pool Reservoir

Use these prompts with Gemini Nano/Banana to create the visual identity for the Hub-and-Spoke model.

## 1. The Central Spring (The Common Pool)
> **Prompt:** A stunning bioluminescent central spring in the heart of a deep forest. Liquid gold nutrients are bubbling up from the earth into a crystal-clear pool. This pool is connected to six surrounding trees via pulsing, underground mycelial pipes. The spring is the source of all light in the scene. Ethereal, sacred geometry, high-tech nature, 8k render.

## 2. The Seniority Waterfall (Priority Filling)
> **Prompt:** A vertical 3D visualization of a multi-tiered fountain. The top tier is a large basin labeled "Common Pool." Liquid light spills over the edges, but it is caught by six smaller basins (The Baselines) below. Only when these six basins are overflowing does the light reach the ground (The Growth Layer). Mechanical elegance meets forest magic, translucent materials.

## 3. The 6-Node Hub (The Network Architecture)
> **Prompt:** An isometric architectural blueprint of a 6-node network. At the center is a glowing holographic orb (The Pool). Six glowing spokes extend from the orb to six distinct futuristic tree structures. Data and nutrients are visibly flowing back and forth along the spokes. Clean, cybernetic-nature aesthetic, blueprints, dark grid background.

## 4. The Exudate Drain (Saturation)
> **Prompt:** A close-up view of a tree trunk with a glowing, semi-transparent tap. Liquid nutrients are flowing *out* of the tree and into a glowing channel that leads back to a central reservoir. The tree looks satisfied and healthy, sharing its excess. Macro photography style, golden liquid, bioluminescence.
